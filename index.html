01 The Why

Why are we building this?

Every year, students create incredible projects. Some solve real problems. Some are creative experiments. Some have the potential to become startups. Yet most of them disappear after evaluation — not because they lack quality, but because they never had a place to continue growing. IIEC Store is being built to change that.

Our goal is simple

To create a space where student-built products can continue evolving beyond the classroom — through discovery, collaboration, and real-world feedback.
